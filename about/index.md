Denzel Otto is a student of Kunstwetenschappen at KU Leuven, a beginning collector, and an occasional writer of essays. This site gathers the two, side by side.

The collection is small and grows slowly. The essays are written in whatever language the thought arrived in — some in Dutch, some in English. There is no attempt to translate between them.
